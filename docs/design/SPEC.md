# Active voxel-3D design

Latest user direction: Minecraft-like clean block geometry, viewed diagonally from above like Civilization. This supersedes the flat SVG map, not the rules. Image generation remains prohibited.

- Off-white chrome #f5f7f3, white cards, ink #29423a. Factions: teal p1 #16846a, red p2 #d14d43, blue p3 #346be0, purple p4 #9651c8, gold city-state #bc8a12, charcoal barbarians #45434a. Apply consistently to terrain borders, icons and unit models. Pale blue exterior sea; uniform parchment on unknown tiles, never actual hidden terrain.
- Real WebGL orthographic map. Hex land columns support cubic stepped hills and mountains, voxel buildings, crop rows, soldiers, horses and cannons. No raster textures or downloaded model assets. Left drag pans, Alt-drag rotates, wheel/pinch zooms. Right click/drag is reserved for unit paths. Soft directional shadows.
- Compact two-row desktop header: wordmark, population/food/production/gold/resources and top-right live clock/pause/end turn/menu; second row has all faction emblems, current turn, relations, direct diplomacy/market. Mobile uses three compact rows. This explicitly supersedes the former single 64px header.
- No permanent right sidebar, no persistent unit-card tray, no dense legend. A compact contextual bottom panel appears only after selection. Detailed stats, production and resources are drawers/popovers on demand.
- Units are original voxel miniatures, with tiny native SVG selection markers for readability and keyboard access. Merged formations show one to three models. Settlers have a packed cart and faction pennant, plus a matching original SVG; builders retain hammers. Settler status and the immediate founding action use the same fixed selection dock, with readable disabled reasons.
- End Turn is beside the clock at the top right, not at the bottom. Compact unit-list toggle bottom left; small optional map-layer and zoom controls. Remember camera position/target/zoom only across state updates and refresh, not strategic observations.
- Korean system sans, 14px body, 12px secondary, deliberate 24px wordmark; 10–16px spacing with ample board space.
- Idle breathing, 520ms per moving hex, curved solid route and own-turn numbered markers. Artillery flight 1400ms, visible impact, victim shake and delayed 1100ms collapse; 3.3-second damage labels and 4-second destruction notice. Synthesized audio settings. Respect reduced-motion and pause rendering when page hidden.
- Functional game controls remain native and keyboard-accessible. Initially fit the complete 20×20 cylindrical world; pan/zoom for detail. Mobile uses a compact bottom sheet without horizontal page overflow. A builder and friendly military occupant have separate offsets and controls.

- Selection shows smooth joined external range contours only: warm white movement and muted red attack, thin support line for contrast, no per-tile highlighting. Narrow pixel-stable HP bars remain visible on observed cities and units, including full health. Ghost contacts have no invented current HP. Unit dock includes allegiance, base/effective attack; enemy hover shows both sides' damage range and modifiers.
- City walls have three bounded levels built with population-based production; display level, city health and firing range. Building shows a hammer animation; melee cavalry/spears never emit artillery shells.
- Civilization dialogs share the bottom anchor and open from either an enemy city or header emblem. Trading uses two columns of selectable cards/chips with add/remove and final review, not raw select forms. The trade window may widen to 780px to keep both sides legible. Human asset/alliance/war deals require explicit consent. Announce war globally with a timed, closable top-layer notice and synthesized cue, including while a modal is open.

All information panels share a fixed bottom anchor and width, including details and production. Tile card sits left of the dock on desktop and directly above on narrow screens. Small city population/queue/ETA badges; clock has active side, tabular seconds and shrinking progress. Animation renders at capped ~30fps through refs, avoiding per-frame React state. Batch repeated blocks using instancing, cap pixel ratio and dispose WebGL resources.

Verification: real WebGL rendering, camera persistence, desktop/mobile screenshots, selection/dock geometry, movement/production, combat forecast, selectable trade cards and real exchange, visible war notice and its modal-safe close button, active audio graph, console errors, no image assets, engine/API regressions. Depth-separate parchment and base tops to avoid zoom flicker. Technical references: https://threejs.org/docs/pages/MapControls.html and https://threejs.org/docs/pages/InstancedMesh.html .
